Theoretical Physics Notes
Double Accretion Disk Lensing
Strong gravitational bending curls light paths from the rear of the accretion disk both over and under the event horizon, forming the iconic double-halo silhouette.
Doppler Asymmetry & Relativistic Beaming
Plasma orbiting towards the camera at relativistic velocities ($v \sim 0.5c$) is blue-shifted and boosted in intensity by $\delta^4$, while plasma moving away is severely dimmed and red-shifted.
Kerr Ergosphere & Frame Dragging
Spin parameter $a > 0$ breaks spherical symmetry, dragging spacetime itself and creating an oblate ergosphere where nothing can remain stationary relative to infinity.
